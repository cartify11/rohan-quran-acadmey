About Our Academy

Empowering Global Muslims Through Quranic Excellence

Online Rohan Quran Academy is dedicated to making quality Quran education accessible to students and families worldwide through personalized one-to-one online classes with male and female Quran tutors.

Founded with the vision of bridging geographical boundaries, our academy connects eager learners of all age groups with qualified Quran tutors. Whether starting from the basic Arabic alphabet or advancing through complete memorization, we offer tailored learning paths suited to each student's pace.

Authentic Tajweed Methodology

Our lessons focus on correct pronunciation (Makharij), rules of Tajweed, and rhythmic Quran recitation.

Experienced Male & Female Tutors

Choose from dedicated male and female Quran teachers specialized in teaching kids, beginners, and adult learners.

Flexible Worldwide Timings

Select class times that easily fit your school, work, or family schedule across any international time zone.
